Common questions

How do I delete my account and all my data?

Open Stones, go to your account (the menu on the Pray screen) and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything you’ve written — for good.

Is my prayer life private?

Yes. Only you can ever see your prayers and journal — never other users, and never anyone signed out. We never sell your data or use tracking. See our privacy policy.

How do I use Stones on a new phone?

Sign in with the same account you used before (Sign in with Apple, or your email). Your prayers, journal and testimonies sync automatically.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password?” and we’ll email you a reset link. (If you signed up with Apple, just use Sign in with Apple again.)

Is Stones free? What’s the tip for?

Stones is free, and every feature stays free. If it’s been a blessing, you can leave an optional tip to support its making — it unlocks nothing.
